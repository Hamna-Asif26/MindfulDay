About MindfulDay

What is MindfulDay?

MindfulDay was created as an AI and automation project to make simple mindfulness activities easier to discover and use in everyday life. It combines a modern website, AI chatbot, and automation features into one accessible platform.

The Problem

People often want to take a mindful break but don't know which activity to choose or where to begin. Without guidance, it's easy to skip these moments entirely.

The Solution

MindfulDay combines a modern website, AI chatbot and automation/integration features into one simple platform — helping you find and practice mindfulness activities that fit your moment.
